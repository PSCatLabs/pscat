Effective Monday, the third floor will begin keeping a daily log of every
visitor who signs the book at the front desk. The log is to be typed, not
written by hand, on the green ruled forms that Supply has sent up, and it is
to be turned in to this office by four o'clock each afternoon.

The reasons are plain. Last quarter we could not say, on two separate
occasions, who had been in the building when a file went missing. Keeping the
book is not enough if no one reads it.

1.	Each entry shows the visitor's name, the hour, and the person he came to
	see, in that order.

2.	A name that cannot be read is to be <x>guessed at</x> <o>queried|checked</o>
	with the desk before it is typed. Do not type a name you are unsure of.

3.	Corrections are made by strikeover, never by erasure, so that the
	original entry <u>remains legible</u> to the reader.

Questions about the new forms should go to Mrs. Okafor, extension 214.
Questions about everything else should go to your supervisor.
