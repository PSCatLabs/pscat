## fmath: one libm on every platform, so seeded renders match (issue #738, 2026-10-25)

**The problem.** `f64::sin`, `exp` and the rest call the platform's libm: Apple's
on macOS, musl's or glibc's on Linux. They round differently in the last bit, and
erosion and noise grow that into visible pixels. fractal_clouds' `mountains` at
`ErN 256 ErDrops 20000`: a Mac and Linux differed in 25,592 of 64,000 pixels
(max 86 levels); `alpine_forest` in 51,400. The Linux musl builds (arm64 and
x86_64) matched each other.

**What was built.** Every transcendental in `src/` goes through `src/fmath/`:

- `sin cos tan asin acos atan atan2 sinh tanh log10 hypot` and the `f32` ones
  (`sinf cosf hypotf`) call the pure-Rust `libm` crate, pinned `=0.2.16`
  (MIT, in `deny.toml`'s allow list; published 2026-01-24).
- `exp ln powf` are a port of musl 1.2.5's (`src/fmath/musl.rs`, tables in
  `src/fmath/tables.rs`, generated by `scripts/gen_fmath_tables.py` from the C
  sources' hex-float literals). The `libm` crate's three are FreeBSD msun's, an
  older design, and are not musl's: used as they are they differ from musl in
  about 5% of results and would have changed every Linux render.
- `rotation()` replaces `Transform::from_rotate`, whose `cos`/`sin` are
  tiny-skia's own platform-libm calls. Every PostScript `rotate` went through it.
- `clippy.toml` bans the direct methods and `from_rotate`, so a new direct call
  fails `cargo clippy`. fmath is the only place allowed to name them.

The wasm/npm build already used `libm`'s algorithms (Rust's wasm std ships a
port), so its `sin cos atan` were musl's all along; its `exp ln pow` change
slightly, to musl's, and now equal macOS and Linux.

**Evidence.**

1. The issue's repro (400 lines x 7 results, `pscat --headless`):

   | pair | differing results of 2,800 |
   |---|---|
   | macOS before vs Linux musl before | 122 (atan 88, cos 17, sin 13, exp 1+3) |
   | macOS after vs Linux musl after | **0** |
   | Linux musl before vs after | **0** |
   | wasm (the npm module, under node) after vs macOS after | **0** |
   | wasm before vs Linux musl before | 103 (all `exp ln pow`: Rust's wasm std ships the older msun versions; `sin cos atan` already matched) |

   (Linux = `rust:alpine`, aarch64, Docker. An intermediate version that used the
   `libm` crate for `exp/ln/pow` too gave 0 between platforms but 103 differences
   from Linux musl before: Linux would have changed.)

2. fractal_clouds `mountains` and `alpine_forest`, Res 0.5 and 0.25, `ErN 256
   ErDrops 20000`, 320x200 at 72 dpi, from a checkout of
   ArtColony/fractal_clouds (`lib/fc_*.ps` as pinned there):

   | | mountains | alpine_forest |
   |---|---|---|
   | macOS before vs Linux before | 25,592 px, max 86 | 51,400 px, max 90 |
   | macOS after vs Linux after | **0** | **0** |
   | Linux before vs Linux after | **0** | **0** |
   | macOS after vs fractal_clouds' stored `tests/regress/linux/*.png` | **0** | **0** |

   The stored Linux references (made with pscat 0.6.2 on Linux) still match, so
   fractal_clouds needs no re-baseline, and a Mac can now run its
   `scripts/regress.sh` without `--docker`.

3. Bit-for-bit against musl 1.2.5 (`scripts/fmath_check.sh`, 4,000,000 random
   inputs per function; `std`'s methods on Alpine are the musl reference):
   `exp ln powf` port, x86_64: 0 differences. aarch64: `exp` 0, `ln` 231 (6 in
   100,000), `powf` 10: musl's arm64 build fuses a multiply-add in `log` and `pow`
   that its x86_64 build does not; the port is the x86_64 form on every target.
   The `libm` crate's `sin cos tan asin acos atan atan2 sinh tanh log10 hypot sinf
   cosf hypotf`: 0 differences on both. Do not use `libm`'s `cosh cbrt log2 exp2`:
   they differ from musl (5-10%), so fmath does not offer them.
   `tests/fmath.rs` pins musl's results for 68 `exp`, 68 `ln` and 80 `pow` inputs
   including zero, infinity, NaN, overflow, subnormals and negative bases.

4. Pixels. The 127 gallery and example renders at 72 dpi (`--max-time 150`), four
   binaries (macOS and Alpine, before and after):

   | pair | images that differ |
   |---|---|
   | macOS before vs Linux before | 11 of 127 (1 to 41 px each) |
   | macOS after vs Linux after | 5 of 127 (1 to 3 px each, max 25 levels at one px) |
   | macOS before vs macOS after | 6 of 127 (2 to 41 px) |
   | **Linux before vs Linux after** | **0 of 127** |

   `scripts/identity_check.sh --base-bin/--head-bin --loader lib/artkit.ps` (101
   pages at gallery 144 dpi), control differs as it must: 95 identical, 6 differ
   by 1 to 167 pixels (`gallery/mochi_denim_blue` 167 of 2.6M, max 12 levels;
   `flying_white_reeds` 47, `paintkit_motion_demo` 7, `paintkit_dry_demo` 6,
   `paintkit_canvas_demo` 3, `evening_stream` 1). The repo's own tests are
   statistical and CI runs on macOS, so no golden moved.

5. Speed: a 1.5M-iteration PostScript loop of `sin`, `ln` and `exp` took 0.67 s
   before and 0.76 s after on a loaded laptop (about 12% on pure math, which is
   not what real pieces spend their time on).

**What is not fixed.** The 5 pieces above that still differ between macOS and
Linux by 1 to 3 pixels. Root cause, measured: tiny-skia's own `f32` `acos`,
`cos` and `powf(1/3)` in its cubic root finder (`chop_cubic_at_max_curvature`,
used when it strokes and hairlines cubics) call the platform libm; a scratch copy
with those calls swapped for `libm` makes all five pixel-identical across
macOS and Linux. Fixing it inside pscat is not possible without forking
tiny-skia (a `[patch]` is ignored for the published crate). Options, for the
owner: an upstream PR to linebender/tiny-skia, or building tiny-skia with
`no-std-float` (needs `std` off, so PNG encode/decode must move to the `png`
crate; and `libm`'s `powf` is not musl's, so Linux would change slightly).

**Gotchas.**
- `Transform::from_rotate` looked innocent. Clippy now bans it.
- On aarch64, `ln` differs from musl on arm64 for 6 in 100,000 inputs (above); a
  Linux arm64 pscat binary and an x86_64 one already differed there, and now
  every platform agrees with x86_64.
- `scripts/selftest.sh` once failed with "lib/drychalk.ps ... is not listed in
  selftest/libraries.txt" under heavy machine load although it is listed, and
  passed on rerun (a `printf | grep -q` under `pipefail` is the likely cause).

`mountains` at Res 0.5, are in `notes/738-fmath-one-libm/`.
