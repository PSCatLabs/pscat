# #351: the strokeglyphs digit 4 no longer reads as a lightning bolt

Found while working #238. Handscript's `/four` is `[430 [280 700 70 250 380 260] [300 480 300 20]]`:
under the midpoint rule the first stroke's interior point `(70 250)` is a
control point, so the diagonal and the crossbar melt into one curved hook
that never reaches `(70 250)`, and the stem is a separate short stroke at a
different x. At board size that is a ϟ.

## What changed

`lib/strokeglyphs.ps` gets a `/four` entry in `sgsrc`. `sginit` copies
handscript's glyphs first and then `sgsrc`, so the entry replaces the copy.
`lib/handscript.ps` is untouched: `hs-write` and every handscript render keep
the old 4 (the existing "hs-write renders identically with strokeglyphs
loaded" test still holds).

    /four [430 [120 700 55 235 55 235 395 235] [295 700 295 10]]

An open 4, as a hand writes it. Stroke one is the left stroke and the
crossbar: it comes down from the top, a little slanted, into a corner at
`(55 235)` written twice so it is sharp (see "Doubled points make corners" in
notes/236), then runs right to `x = 395`. Stroke two is a straight stem, full
height, from `(295 700)` to the baseline, crossing the crossbar; the crossbar
runs 100 units past it. The two strokes do not meet at the top. The advance
stays 430, so no line's layout moves: every pixel that differs in the renders
is inside a 4 (checked against all the changed regions).

**Review round.** The first version was a closed (triangle) 4, the diagonal
and the stem meeting at the top. The maintainer preferred an open 4 that is
more readable as a four. I rendered nine variants side by side at board size
(chalkboard 4!, theta^4, "Oct 14", the digits row, at /Jitter 0 and with the
default jitter), varying how vertical the left stroke is (top x from 70 to
150, corner x from 40 to 70), the crossbar height (235 to 250) and the stem
x (285 to 300). All the open forms read as a 4 and none as a bolt; the one
kept has a slight slant (a vertical left stroke makes "4" look like "H" with a
missing leg, a strong slant looks like the old hook), a clear corner, and a
crossbar that visibly crosses a full-height stem.

The superscript and subscript 4s are derived from the base glyph in `sginit`
(notes/339), so `x⁴`, `₄` and mathkit's `^4` / `_4` follow for free.

A `%%SelfTest: digit-four-is-an-open-four` pins the advance, the bbox, the
crossbar-past-the-stem property, and the open top (the first stroke starts at
`(120, 700)`, left of the stem).

## Evidence

PR #421 (#298) landed on main while this was in review and also regenerated
`chalkboard_lecture.png` and both strokeglyphs specimens (its 7). The merge
conflicted on those PNGs; they were re-rendered from the merged tree rather
than picking a side, and the identity check and the before/after panel below
are against merged main (c9b53b2). The 7 therefore looks the same in both
halves of the panel; only the 4s differ. `lecture_compare_238.png` was not
regenerated by #421, so it is rebuilt here from the new still.

`scripts/identity_check.sh --loader lib/strokeglyphs.ps` against a detached
`origin/main` worktree, one binary for both trees: of the nine files that load
strokeglyphs, exactly these differ, and the rest are byte-identical.

- DIFFERS (every changed pixel cluster is a 4; the panel shows all of them): `gallery/chalkboard_lecture.ps`, `examples/mathkit_specimen.ps`,
  `examples/strokeglyphs.ps`, `examples/strokeglyphs_subscripts_greek.ps`,
  `selftest/drivers/mathkit.ps` page 1.
- identical: `gallery/crayon_drawing.ps` (it writes only the capitals of a
  name), `examples/drychalk_specimen.ps`,
  `examples/strokeglyphs_board_math.ps`, every other driver page.

The base tree reproduced each committed PNG byte for byte with the commands
in the files' headers, so regenerating them with the same commands adds no
unrelated change. Regenerated:

- `gallery/renders/chalkboard_lecture.png` (`--page 1440x850 --dpi 144`)
- `docs/drymedia/mathkit_specimen.png` (`--page 1000x1960 --dpi 144`)
- `docs/drymedia/strokeglyphs_specimen.png` (`--page 900x1600`)
- `docs/drymedia/strokeglyphs_subscripts_greek_specimen.png` (`--page 900x600 --dpi 144`)
- `docs/drymedia/lecture_compare_238.png`, rebuilt with
  `prototypes/lecture_compare.py` from the new still (its inputs are in the
  repo; the base tree's output matched the committed file exactly, so only

The Ghostscript renders (`crayon_drawing_gs.png`, `drychalk_specimen_gs.png`)
come from files whose pscat render did not change, so they were left. The
chalkboard has no committed gs render.

`docs/drymedia/four_compare_351.png` is the before/after panel: every 4 that
changed, left is before, right is after.

Timing guard, pscat only: chalkboard about 7 to 9 s at 144 dpi before and
after, mathkit specimen about 1 s, the strokeglyphs specimens under 1 s. The
local machine is noisy, so these are not budgets.

## Not done

The 4 is the only glyph changed. The 7 (`[70 700 360 700 180 20]`) has the
same melted-corner look in the chalkboard crops ("1748" reads as "1)48"), but
that is a different glyph, fixed separately by #298 / PR #421 (which doubles the 7's corner in handscript). #410 was filed here before that was noticed.
