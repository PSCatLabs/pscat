## render: exact-area coverage for fills, so sub-pixel shapes keep their gray (PR #778, issue #692, 2026-10-25)

- **What changed / what to look at:** fills and glyphs now get exact-area anti-aliasing (`src/coverage.rs`) instead of tiny-skia's 4x4 supersampling. Shapes thinner than a pixel keep a gray proportional to their area instead of vanishing or snapping to one gray. Re-measured on the merged tree (main at 56006db): 137 of 176 corpus pages move, mostly at edges; the stroke-only pages and every `selftest/drivers/image_*.ps` driver (the new `image_alpha` included) are identical. The `pscat chalk --no-board` overlay from #713 moves too (its pin is re-pinned): its specks keep their area, and the √ bar and superscript stay solid (last row). Merged by the agent under tier (b), before the 0.7.0 cut (the timing the owner picked on 2026-10-26).
- **Perceptual check:** "Candidate C ..., rendered directly at 48 px, reads as a shaded cat, not stripes. A gray ramp of rules from 0.05 to 1 px looks like a smooth ramp, not two or three bands. Existing gallery renders at normal size look unchanged, or better."
- **Reviewer:** PASS, round 2 on the merged tree (https://github.com/PSCatLabs/pscat/pull/778#issuecomment-6425669532; round 1, before the merge of main: https://github.com/PSCatLabs/pscat/pull/778#issuecomment-6406228925). polish: faint banding in the logo's body is still just visible at 6x, gone at 48 px.
