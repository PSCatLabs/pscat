Learn PostScript

A language where you push values onto a stack and then say what to do with them.

The full tutorial is still being written: the history of PostScript, a stack-language primer, and programs you can run as you read. This page holds its place in the navigation, with a first taste in the meantime.

A first taste

PostScript puts the operands first and the operator last. In 100 100 moveto, the two numbers are pushed onto the stack and moveto pops them and moves the pen there. Drawing is describing a path, then painting it. This fills a teal square:

%!PS
0.2 0.7 0.6 setrgbcolor
100 100 moveto  200 100 lineto  200 200 lineto  100 200 lineto  closepath
fill
showpage

Paste it into the playground and press Run. The playground opens on a longer program that you can edit, and its example menu loads more.
